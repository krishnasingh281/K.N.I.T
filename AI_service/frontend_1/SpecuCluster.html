Split by what each node can actually do.

Every node reports its layer range and health to the orchestrator. Layers are partitioned in proportion to capacity, and the scene follows whatever the registry says. A throttled browser tab gets fewer layers. An idle GPU gets more.

Layers per node, live. The stacks in the scene resize with it.

Draft ahead. Verify in one pass.

A small draft model proposes a block of tokens. The target model checks the whole block in one batched pass, keeps what it agrees with, corrects the first miss and throws away the rest.

Nodes drop. Generation doesn’t.

Drop a node and its layers are handed to the survivors while the loop keeps running. Bring it back and they flow home.

Three disciplines, one loop.

Speculative decoding and adaptive tensor sharding. A gRPC and WebSocket transport that streams activations and KV-cache between nodes. A Next.js control plane with a WebGL memory graph and hot-swappable nodes.
